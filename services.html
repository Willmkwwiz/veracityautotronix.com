Professional Battery Testing

Accurate diagnostics to ensure your battery delivers reliable performance when you need it most.

1
Visual Inspection

Check for physical damage, terminal corrosion, and electrolyte levels.

2
Voltage Measurement

Digital multimeter test — healthy 12V battery should read 12.6V+ when fully charged.

3
Load & CCA Testing

Apply load equal to half CCA rating. Voltage must stay above 9.6V during test.

4
Charging System Check

Verify alternator output (13.5–14.5V) to ensure proper charging while driving.

Expert Battery Charging

Safe, multi-stage charging to restore battery health and extend its lifespan significantly.

1
Safety Preparation

Remove battery if needed, ensure ventilation, and wear protective gear.

2
Terminal Cleaning

Clean terminals and connectors for optimal electrical contact and conductivity.

3
Smart Multi-Stage Charging

Using advanced chargers: Bulk, Absorption, and Float maintenance modes.

4
Final Verification

Post-charge voltage and load test to confirm full battery restoration.
