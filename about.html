Our Story

Who We Are

Veracity Autotronix, operating as the Willard Battery Center, is a leading automotive battery supplier located in Area 4, Lilongwe, Malawi. Strategically positioned off Paul Kagame Road near Park View Centre, we are accessible to drivers across the city.

As an authorized stockist and dealer of Willard Batteries, we bring Malawi the most trusted battery brand from South Africa. Willard has been setting standards since the 1920s, with local South African production established in 1954 — and became the first battery brand to receive the SABS Mark of Approval.

Beyond Willard, we carry a curated selection of premium brands — GP Batteries, SABAT, and TOPLITE — to cover every vehicle type, from family cars and commercial trucks to agricultural equipment and motorcycles.

Our mission is simple: provide genuine products, expert advice, and honest service to every customer who walks through our doors.
